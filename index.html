Sustainable Living

Jute & Natural Materials

Celebrated globally as the "Golden Fiber", Bangladesh jute is one of the strongest, most sustainable plant fibers on earth.

Our golden jute totes, braided storage baskets, and round floor mats are handwoven by artisan cooperatives in rural Bangladesh — blending organic durability with modern coastal Australian styling.

  • 100% Biodegradable & Carbon-Negative: Naturally renewable crop nurtured by monsoon river deltas.
  • Durable & Heavy-Duty: Braided weave engineered for daily shopping, storage, and home use.
  • Coastal Aesthetic: Natural golden tones that complement Australian wood and linen decors.
